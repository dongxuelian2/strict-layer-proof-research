# Fourth 85 · R4 — \(K=10\) q=1 Local Closure Certificate

For the normalized R4 norm torsor

\[
x^2-A_2(G,10)v^2=T_4(G,10),
\]

take \(G=10^g\).

Exact congruences:

\[
A_2(10^g,10)\equiv2\pmod3,
\]

and

\[
T_4(10^g,10)\equiv27\pmod{81}.
\]

Thus \(A_2\) is a nonsquare 3-adic unit and

\[
v_3(T_4)=3.
\]

Therefore \(\mathbf Q_3(\sqrt{A_2})/\mathbf Q_3\) is unramified quadratic, while the required norm has odd 3-adic valuation. Impossible.

Hence:

\[
\boxed{
K=10,\ q=1,\ \text{negative branch}\Longrightarrow\varnothing.
}
\]

All eight historical \((d,\tau)\) cases with \(K=10\) are closed.

This is a partial q=1 closure only; \(K=100,1000\) remain open.
