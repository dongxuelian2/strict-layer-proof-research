\[
N_{\mathbf Q(i)/\mathbf Q}(\mathbf Q(i)^\times).
\]
Equivalently it is represented by the fixed principal binary quadratic form x^2+y^2 of discriminant -4.

### Q2 — Is split exactly Gaussian norm / square class?
YES for the rational G1 split criterion:
\[
(-1,N_0)=0
\iff
N_0\in N_{\mathbf Q(i)/\mathbf Q}(\mathbf Q(i)^\times)
\iff
v_p(N_0)\equiv0\pmod2\quad\forall p\equiv3\pmod4.
\]
Because N0 is a positive integer, this is also equivalent to integral representation as x^2+y^2. This existence equivalence does not identify rational and integral norm problems in general; it is special to this positive integral target.
