# 75-R5 Terminal Verdict

\[
\boxed{
\texttt{R5_TERMINAL_VERDICT=SOURCE_SPECIFIC_ADVANTAGE_UNRESOLVED}
}
\]

The verdict is deliberately not `SOURCE_SPECIFIC_EXPONENT_IMPROVEMENT`: R5 proves a genuine **local-\(u\)** exponent improvement, but not yet a total joint-cost exponent improvement because the exact allowed-ruling source basepoint height remains uncontrolled.

## Decisive R5 results

1. \(x=\pm Z+u\lambda\) extracts one factor \(u\) but leaves a genuine quadratic lift.
2. The source lattice and the \(u\)-ruling are whole-modulus compatible; taking \(Z=1\) costs only one class \(a\bmod M_s\).
3. R17/R18 composition does not supply a source point constructor.
4. Conditional on an exact allowed-ruling basepoint, the R20 transverse Veronese chart satisfies
   \[
   E=O(j(u))=G^{o(1)}.
   \]
5. For the same chart,
   \[
   D\le a_p\mathcal H_{\perp,\tau},
   \]
   so the relevant basepoint quantity is the source digit denominator \(a_p\), not its full Euclidean height.
6. The old local factor \(u^2\) in the joint cost is replaced by \(j(u)^2\).
7. A chart-independent squarefree-discriminant lower-bound mechanism is identified.
8. The old integer quantization \(R_{\rm LOW}\gtrsim G^{-1}\) cannot by itself collide with a cubic threshold whose absolute best scale is \(G^{-1/3}\).
