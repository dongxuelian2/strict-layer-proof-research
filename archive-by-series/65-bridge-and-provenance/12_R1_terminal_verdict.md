## Verdict 5 — What exactly remains new mathematics?

After importing the mature pieces, the minimal genuinely project-specific obligations are:

### N1 — Actual split/nonsplit theorem
Decide the Gaussian norm/Brauer class on the exact power-of-ten source family, rather than on an ambient family.

### N2 — Source-semantic moving height theorem
On a split fibre, establish a primitive source point in the **moving decimal radial/digit shell**, simultaneously with the source integral model. This is narrower than the old R19 finite-orbit theorem: finite packet membership is already retired.

### N3 — Reciprocity audit for N2
Prove that the actual source finite data and real digit sector do not carry a hidden reciprocity obstruction of the type visible in primitive ternary cones, or identify the obstruction if they do.

### N4 — Exact power-ten algebraicization

q=1 remains independent.
