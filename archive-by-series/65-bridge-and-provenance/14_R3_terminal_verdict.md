# 75-R3 Terminal Verdict

\[
\boxed{
\texttt{R3_TERMINAL_VERDICT=USSPAL_ELEMENTARY_REDUCTION}
}
\]

The reduction is stronger than R2 in four precise ways:

1. fixed-data primitive sector approximation is proved elementarily on \(\mathbf P^1\);
2. primitive extraction has no height penalty;
3. \(M_0q^2\) is excluded from the USSPAL local level;
4. the moving constant is compressed to a source splitting-distortion factor times an allowed-\(u\)-class cost, with an explicit cubic LOW-degeneration inequality.

USSPAL itself is not yet globally proved because \(\mathfrak D_\tau\) has no uniform power-ten bound.
