# 75-R2 Terminal Verdict

## Main verdict

\[
\boxed{\texttt{R2_TERMINAL_VERDICT=SOURCE_HEIGHT_BRIDGE_REDUCED}}
\]

P2 reaches M4 completely and M5 by criterion **M5-B**: N2 is reduced to the explicit Uniform Source-Sector Primitive Approximation Lemma (USSPAL), strictly smaller than the original q>1 split-fibre frontier.

The reduction is not a proof of USSPAL and therefore is not `SOURCE_HEIGHT_THEOREM_IMPORTED`.

## Strategic verdict

The punctured source cone migration is semantically valid. The correct external architecture is not “one toric strong approximation theorem solves everything”, but
\[
\boxed{
\text{source dilatation}
\to \mathbb G_m\text{-torsor/projective conic}
\to \text{quantitative primitive-ray distribution}
\to \text{R14 radial multiplier}
\to \text{R20 reconstruction}.}
\]

The main surviving P2 obstacle is the uniformity of quantitative distribution when the form, source lattice/local level and Archimedean sector all move with \(G=10^g\).
