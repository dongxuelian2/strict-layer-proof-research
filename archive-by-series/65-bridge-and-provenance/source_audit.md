1. Colliot-Thélène–Xu, arXiv:0712.1957 / Compositio Math. 145 (2009).
   - Theorem 3.7 exact hypotheses: number field, S-integers, semisimple simply connected G, connected H, separated finite-type integral model with generic fibre G/H.
   - Proof cites Platonov–Rapinchuk English ed. §7.4, Thm. 7.12, p.427 for strong approximation.
   - Theorems 6.1, 6.3 checked.

2. Mayeux–Richarz–Romagny, arXiv:2001.03597v4.
   - Definition 2.1 affine blow-up algebra/dilatation.
   - \(B[I/b]\subset B[b^{-1}]\), generic complement unchanged.
   - Lemmas 2.3–2.4 and Intro theorem properties checked.

3. Cao–Xu, Ann. Inst. Fourier 68 (2018), 1879–1908.
   - Theorem 1.2: smooth toric varieties satisfy SA with BM obstruction off infinity.
   - Explicit warning immediately after theorem: proof cannot force prescribed real connected components.

4. Huang, arXiv:2512.13091v1.
   - \(W^\circ\) primitive integral interpretation checked.
   - \(\Omega=8L\Delta\) checked.
   - Theorem 1.3 printed “Assume \(\Omega\mid L\)” checked.
   - Example 1.4 real-sign / finite-congruence reciprocity obstruction checked.
   - \(W^\circ\) as smooth toric \(\mathbb G_m\)-torsor over conic checked.

5. O'Dorney, arXiv:2205.12829v2 / Proc. AMS 151 (2023).
   - integral conic / quaternionic subring / invariant lattice framework verified from abstract/full HTML metadata.

6. Evertse–Schlickewei–Schmidt, Annals 155 (2002), 807–836.

7. Ferrand, Bull. SMF 131 (2003), 553–585.
   - Numdam abstract verifies conductor fiber-product/descent result §2.2 and pinching existence §5.4.
