# 75-R5 — Remaining New Mathematics

R5 compresses the remaining USSPAL mathematics to two sharply dual statements.

## P — Allowed-Ruling Source Denominator Theorem

Produce, uniformly on every relevant split fibre, a primitive exact source point

\[
p=(Z_p,a_p,x_p)
\]

with

\[
Q_\tau(p)=0,
\qquad
p\in L_{\rm source},
\qquad
Z_p\in(\mathbb Z/u)^\times,
\qquad
x_p\equiv\pm Z_p\pmod u,
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
a_p\mathcal H_{\perp,\tau}
\le
G^{1-\delta+o(1)}
}
\]

for some fixed \(\delta>0\).

This would immediately give

\[
J_\tau=G^{1-\delta+o(1)}
\]

up to Jacobsthal factors and hence a genuine shrinking Ultra-LOW locus.

## N — Uniform intrinsic chart-height barrier

Prove, for example, a strong uniform lower bound on

\[
\operatorname{sf}(N_0-1)
\]

or an equivalent source-content/discriminant invariant which forces

\[
D(\Phi)\ge G^{1-o(1)}
\]

for every good source chart.

Such a theorem would give a structural negative verdict for the entire USSPAL chart strategy.

## Not remaining

The following are no longer independent R5 problems:

- primitive ray distribution on \(\mathbf P^1\);
- full \(M_0q^2\) packet matching;
- generic Cassels small-zero existence;
- full integral Witt-frame construction;
- separate optimization of \(D\) and \(E\);
- prime-by-prime \(u\)-open classification.
