# 75-R5 — USSPAL Route Viability

## Positive result

The full allowed \(u\)-ruling geometry is quantitatively useful.

Once an exact allowed-ruling source basepoint exists, the R20 source Veronese chart admits a transversal for which the entire finite-open condition reduces to

\[
(s-\sigma M_st,u)=1.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
E(\Phi)=O(j(u))=G^{o(1)}.
}
\]

This removes the old polynomial local penalty \(u\).

## Negative result

No internal constructor from R13–R20 yields a uniformly low \(a_p\)-height allowed-ruling point.

The natural exact substitution

\[
x=\pm Z+u\lambda
\]

remains genuinely quadratic.

R17/R18 composition does not act as a source-lattice point constructor.

A generic congruence-small-zero migration is strategically unsuitable because its modulus dependence reintroduces a positive power of \(u\).

## Survival verdict

The route deserves **one more sharply focused campaign**, but not another generic height-estimate round.

The next campaign must decide one of two mutually useful statements:

### Positive theorem
\[
a_p\mathcal H_{\perp,\tau}
\le
G^{1-\delta+o(1)}
\]

for an allowed-ruling exact source point.

### Negative theorem
A uniform intrinsic lower bound showing that every good chart satisfies

\[
D(\Phi)\gtrsim G^{1-o(1)}
\]

or stronger.

If neither can be reached after attacking exact source content and the squarefree discriminant, the USSPAL chart interface should be retired.
