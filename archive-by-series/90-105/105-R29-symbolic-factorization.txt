1. Xi exact expansion

u0*Xi = W*(Q0-P3) + A*10^n*(10^g*Q0-P2)
      = W*Q0 - W*P3 + A*10^(n+g)*Q0 - A*10^n*P2.

With P2=u0*W*C2 and P3=u0*A*C3:

u0*Xi = Q0*(W+A*10^(n+g)) - u0*A*W*(C3+10^n*C2).

mu*[Q0*ell-AW(C3+10^n*C2)] = a0*10^r*(10^k P1-Q0).

Move g1*W*T to the right:
A*W*u0*X*Y*G*D = g1*(W*T+A*Y*H),
which is exactly R28-PF TC1.
Thus TC1_CONDITIONED_DENOMINATOR_RATIO_INFORMATION_GAIN = 0 on the post-support/master locus.
