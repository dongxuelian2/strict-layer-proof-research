J2-65 R8 EXACT NORM FORMS
FIELD F = Q(q,X,lambda)
r=T/alpha, y=Y/alpha

A(r)=A+B*r+C*r^2
L(r)=E+F*r
z=2*D*y+lambda*L(r)

z^2=a*r^2+b*r+c
a=lambda^2*F^2-4*D*C
b=2*lambda^2*E*F-4*D*B
c=lambda^2*E^2-4*D*A

w=2*a*r+b
Delta_B=b^2-4*a*c
w^2-4*a*z^2=Delta_B

MOVING NORM PRESENTATION:
E_moving=F(sqrt(a))
N(w+2*z*sqrt(a))=Delta_B
[Delta_B]=[-Xi]

FIXED TORUS PRESENTATION:
H=4*A*C-B^2=[2*q*(X+1)*(q+4)*c]^2*R
J_A=2*A*F-B*E
V=2*A*alpha+B*T+lambda*E*Y
W=H*T+lambda*J_A*Y
W^2+H*V^2=-16*A*det(M_P+lambda*M_S)*Y^2

For actual q>1, Y!=0:
E_fixed=F(sqrt(-H))=F(sqrt(-R))
N(W/Y+(V/Y)*sqrt(-H))=-16*A*det(M)

A=X*A1, h=2*X+q+2
beta=(-R,-A1*h*Xi)

Cyclotomic pullback X=u*q-1, lambda=K/X:
R=q^2*rho
A1=q*sigma
h=q*(2u+1)
Xi=q^2*Phi
beta_act=(-rho,-sigma*(2u+1)*Phi)

rho = 4*q**2*u**4 + 4*q**2*u**3 + q**2*u**2 - 8*q*u**3 - 4*q*u**2 + 4*u**2 - 2
sigma = 2*q**4*u**3 + q**4*u**2 + 16*q**3*u**3 + 4*q**3*u**2 + 32*q**2*u**3 - 8*q**2*u**2 + 4*q**2*u - 32*q*u**2 + 8*q*u + 8*u - 4
Phi = -4*K**2*q**2*u**4 + 8*K**2*q*u**3 - 4*K**2*u**2 + 4*q**2*u**4 + 4*q**2*u**3 + q**2*u**2 - 8*q*u**3 - 4*q*u**2 + 4*u**2 - 2
